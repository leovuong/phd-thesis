Title
- no Abbreviation in it
- upper case each word or sentence case (be consistent choose one)

Font 
Text -size 10
Figure text -size 8

Standard text settings: 
Arial 10 justify 
1.5 lines line spacing
spacing before 12 pt
spacing after 0 pt 

Margin
3 cm on the outside 
4 cm on the inside
- show margin line (File > Options > Advanced > Under show document content -> tick Show text boundaries)
- with this setting of margins, figure width should be set max. 14 cm.

Figure
- have text that refer to each figure not just figure
- figure text should be smaller font size than text.
- put "." after Figure number
- Figures should be standalone, so don’t assume abbreviations are known. 
- Every abbreviation in a figure and its legend should be explained.
- automatically add figure number (References > Under Captions->Insert caption), and to add figure with number in text (References > Under Captions->Cross-reference)
  : To update all fields (Ctrl+A, then F9)

List of abbreviation
- put the special symbols at the end of the list
- no plurals, only singular (e.g., AUC and not AUCs)

Writing style
- British or American english

Checklist 
- add page number before printing
- update page number in TOC
- check for double space (ctrl + f -> "  ")
- check ordering of table and figure number
- et al, a priori, a posteriori in the text should be in italics
- abbrviation: abbrviate again in every new chapters (optinal)
- shift+enter to put sentence on the same line
- check text alignment
