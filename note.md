# Guidelines layout thesis manuscript
- There are no guidelines with regard to font size, linespace, etc.
- Check the regulations on Intellectual Property Rights; copyright; plagiarism; non disclosure; authorship, open access, etc.
- The doctoral dissertation may be written in English or Dutch. 
- The thesis manuscript needs to be in A4 reading format for review by your jury members.
- The final thesis booklet has a default width of 16 cm and default height of 24 cm. Take this into account when selecting the size of the font and the spacing.
    - Implications?
- Deviations are possible after your motivated request has been approved by the Doctoral School
